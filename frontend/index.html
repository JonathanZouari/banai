AI Designer

Describe the wardrobe you want, and the agent will propose changes for you to confirm.

The conversational design agent is not connected yet (planned for Phase 5). You can design manually in the Properties panel.

Getting started

  • Drag to rotate the wardrobe.
  • Right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
  • Click a part to edit it.
  • Press Esc or click empty space to clear the selection.
  • Use the interior switch to see the shelves.
